About me

I’ve been hunting since 2016, with 79 valid reports resolved across telecoms, finance, automotive, government and media programs. My strength is scale: deep reconnaissance over large, sprawling attack surfaces, then turning an overlooked asset or an unpatched known-CVE into a proven, high-impact vulnerability.

I’ve found the same class of bug across hundreds of hosts — DOM XSS through exposed Swagger UI, SSRF via vulnerable WebLogic and Apache, Jira and OpenAPI information disclosure, and subdomain takeovers on dangling cloud records — as well as logic flaws like payment-gateway clickjacking, OTP user enumeration, and identity-verification bypass.

I care about clear reports: reproducible steps, honest impact, and a concrete fix, so triage teams can close the root cause, not just the symptom.

  • ReconnaissanceAsset discovery, subdomain enumeration at scale
  • Known-CVE exploitationWebLogic, Apache, Jira, Swagger UI
  • Cross-site scriptingDOM, reflected & stored XSS, CSRF chains
  • Server-sideSSRF, path & info disclosure
  • Subdomain takeoverNetlify, Shopify, Hatena, dangling DNS
  • Logic & identityClickjacking, rate-limit, KYC bypass
